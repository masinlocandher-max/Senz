Who does what

Clear roles protect you and your customers.

You, the partner

  • Build and manage your own brand
  • Find and manage your own customers
  • Use approved sales resources
  • Request eligible white-label products or scoped fulfilment
  • Pay agreed supplier charges
  • Handle first-line customer communication within your agreements

SENZ, the supplier

  • Maintains the approved catalog
  • Confirms feasibility, supplier pricing, capacity and scope
  • Supplies licensed products or contracted production
  • Handles its supplier obligations within published terms
How fulfilment works

A request becomes an order only when both sides agree.

  1. You write a brief

    The customer’s problem, requirements, timing and budget, in business terms.

  2. SENZ reviews

    Feasibility, supplier price, capacity and scope. SENZ may ask for more information or decline.

  3. You accept a quotation

    Only then does SENZ commit to supply. A submitted brief is not a commitment.

  4. Production to delivery

    Customer review against agreed acceptance rules, delivery, then support within the stated scope.

The default arrangement is reseller fulfilment: you contract and invoice your customer, and SENZ supplies you. Any direct-contract referral arrangement is offered separately and never mixed into a reseller order.

Plainly stated

What partnership is, and is not.

It is

  • An independent business you own and run
  • Access to approved SENZ products and production
  • Written quotations before any order

It is not

  • Employment with SENZ, or a franchise
  • A supply of customers or guaranteed earnings
  • A business registration or a licence to SENZ intellectual property
  • Unlimited human support
