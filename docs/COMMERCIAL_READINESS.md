# Commercial readiness

Senzapp proves capability; Senz decides whether and how it is sold. [SENZ commercial catalog](https://github.com/masinlocandher-max/Senz/blob/main/commercial/catalog.json) is authoritative for public offers and approved prices. [Pricing audit](https://github.com/masinlocandher-max/Senz/blob/main/docs/commercial/PRICING_AUDIT.md) records withdrawn historical amounts and missing approval evidence.

Current products are quote/request paths only. Public list prices are null, commercial approval is false and production verification is false. Do not charge based on a historical source amount. Named plans and configured allowances are not commercial approval. A real signed quote must identify currency, period, seats, usage, setup, renewal, taxes and add-ons.

No public checkout or automated payment verification is implemented. Private app staff record verified authorization and unique references; signup, membership, quote acceptance and submitted activation requests never grant access alone. Pilots require explicit scope and authorization and do not establish general availability.

Promotion requires the technical registry, deployment evidence, commercial approval, pricing/quote terms, delivery process and policies to pass the shared contract gate. See [release and integration rules](https://github.com/masinlocandher-max/Senz/blob/main/docs/architecture/ECOSYSTEM.md). Live inbox receipt, payment reconciliation, application infrastructure, backups/restore and any billed AI need their own evidence.
