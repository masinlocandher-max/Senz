# One SENZ experience

SENZ is the strategic communications and digital solutions brand within FMB&CO. The main site helps clients commission work. The Partner Program helps independent entrepreneurs shape and deliver their own offers with eligible SENZ products and resources. The program is ongoing.

## Shared interface

Keep senz-system.css identical between repositories (components/ on SENZ, app/ on Partner). Use the SENZ mark, Montserrat, navy #0a1730, blue #2451d9, pale backgrounds, pill buttons, the same six-item global navigation and FMB&CO endorsement. Partner pages add a secondary navigation for builder, catalog, membership and workspace. Every routed page uses the shared header/footer and a keyboard skip link.

## Conversion journey

1. Promise a concrete outcome: prepare your own digital-solutions offer.
2. Choose a category and view its actual availability.
3. Personalise a business concept using name, customer market and delivery preference.
4. Keep the free plan and introduction before any paid decision.
5. Inspect useful samples.
6. Compare free tools with the 14-document membership kit, saved drafts, exports and checklist. Show ₱999 and separate product costs together.

Use useful outputs, clear scope and visible progress to build confidence. Do not add fake testimonials, countdowns, earnings guarantees or implied live product access. Online checkout stays disabled until payment and commercial terms are approved. The membership CTA opens an enquiry; it does not activate membership.
