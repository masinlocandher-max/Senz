## Unit economics and limitations

One AI credit covers up to USD 0.01 of provider cost, rounded up per successful call. Reserve a conservative upper bound before the call; settle to actual provider usage and return unused allowance. Failed calls refund the customer allowance. Existing paid licenses retain their original action metering and allowance. Calendar-month allowances do not roll over.

The new AI allowances target about 20% maximum model cost at an internal planning assumption of PHP 65/USD (a buffer, not a quoted exchange rate). This leaves the balance for hosting, payment costs, tax, support and margin; it is not a guaranteed profit. Individual/Professional/Business/Institutional allowances are 900/1700/750/450 credits per seat. Review measured provider cost and support effort after a pilot. Workflow products compete with free tools; demonstrate the integrated workflow and support value before treating their prices as validated. Do not promise unlimited usage or included professional services.
