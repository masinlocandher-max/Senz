What we solve

Start with the problem. Build only what solves it.

Most organizations do not need more output. They need the right piece of work, done properly.

  • People cannot find you, or cannot tell what you offer.

    Visitors arrive, hesitate and leave without asking.

    Websites and digital presence. Clear pages, honest copy, search foundations and an enquiry path that turns visits into conversations.

    Websites and digital presence

  • Your reputation is not keeping up with your work.

    The public story is thinner, or different, from the real one.

    Strategic communications, PR and reputation. Positioning, messaging, media and reputation work so people understand why you matter.

    Communications and PR

  • Your team repeats the same manual work every day.

    Enquiries, bookings and records move between chats, sheets and memory.

    Applications and scoped automation. Portals, booking systems, dashboards and human-reviewed automation built around how you already work.

    Applications and automation

  • Every campaign looks and sounds different.

    Visuals, videos and posts do not add up to one message.

    Content videos and marketing kits. One message carried through coordinated videos and campaign materials, with usage rights agreed up front.

    Content and marketing kits

Working with SENZ

A simple path from first conversation to finished work.

  1. Free Authority Check

    Up to 30 minutes to find the gap that matters most. Diagnosis, not unpaid production.

  2. A defined scope

    If there is a fit, you receive the smallest useful scope with a written quotation.

  3. Build and launch

    Agreed deliverables, review points and acceptance, done properly.

  4. Support when useful

    Ongoing care or growth work only when there is something worth maintaining.

Founder

Built so more Filipinos can own what they build.

Francine Marie Bautista is a Filipina brand strategist, public relations practitioner, creative director, storyteller, photographer and trainer. She founded SENZ to help valuable people and ideas become easier to understand and harder to dismiss.

Her purpose is simple: help more Filipinos take part in technology and build something of their own, whether that is a clearer brand for a business that already exists or a new digital firm under their own name.
