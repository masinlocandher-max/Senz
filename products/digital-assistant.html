Who it is for

Businesses, schools, clinics, venues, and organizations with repeat questions.

The problem it solves

People keep asking the same questions, and answers are slow or inconsistent.

What you can do

  • An approved-knowledge library that you control
  • Answers that cite their sources; unanswerable questions become handoff requests to your team
  • An embed code that works only on the websites you allow, with pause and unpublish at any time

What is outside the scope

It replies only from your approved knowledge: an answer must cite it, and every number, date, price, or contact detail must appear in what it cites, or the question goes to your team. It does not take payments or make bookings.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱2,990 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.
