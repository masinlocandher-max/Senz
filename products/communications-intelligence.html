Who it is for

Communications officers, schools, local governments, agencies, and organizations.

The problem it solves

Important messages go out unplanned, unclear, or without proper approval.

What you can do

  • Six structured tools: message brief, stakeholder plan, clarity and tone review, campaign narrative, issue and crisis preparation, and communications diagnostic
  • Human approval required before export; every new version needs approval again
  • Optional SENZ strategic review of a version you choose to share

What is outside the scope

It does not publish or send messages, and it does not replace legal advice or a crisis team.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.
