Who it is for

Small sales teams, service businesses, and professionals.

The problem it solves

Leads and customers are tracked in chats and notebooks, and follow-ups are forgotten.

What you can do

  • Contacts with deal value, stage, next follow-up date, and a dated notes history
  • Pipeline and follow-up views with overdue flags, search, and totals
  • Add an inquiry or booking request to the CRM in one step; export contacts as CSV

What is outside the scope

It does not send emails or messages and does not import contacts from other systems.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱1,290 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.
