SENZ Booking

Online Appointment Booking.

Availability requests are open. SENZ will confirm when your product can be activated and what you will pay before sending payment instructions.

Who it is for

Salons, clinics, studios, consultants, and other appointment-based businesses.

The problem it solves

Appointment requests arrive by chat and phone, and the same time gets promised to two people.

What you can do

  • Services with durations, open days and start times, closed dates, and optional staff
  • A public booking page and QR code; you confirm or decline each request, and a confirmed time cannot be given twice
  • Customers check or cancel a request with its reference; you add phone and walk-in bookings yourself

What is outside the scope

It does not take payments or deposits and sends no messages or reminders; you contact customers yourself.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱990 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.

Need SENZ to do the work with you?

Software access and professional services have separate scopes. For strategy, PR, creative direction or a custom implementation, discuss a service project.
