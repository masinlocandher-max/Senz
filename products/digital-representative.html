Who it is for

Public figures, brands, and organizations under a signed agreement.

The problem it solves

A brand or public figure receives more questions than can be answered personally.

What you can do

  • Recorded, revocable consent with an evidence reference
  • Answers only from approved knowledge, always disclosed as automated
  • Publication stops immediately when consent is revoked

What is outside the scope

Text only. It has no voice or video likeness and is never presented as the real person.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱7,900 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.
