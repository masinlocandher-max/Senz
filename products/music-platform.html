Who it is for

Artists, producers, and clients working with SENZ under a music project agreement.

The problem it solves

Music projects are shared through scattered links, with unclear feedback and unconfirmed rights.

What you can do

  • Protected audio previews with version history
  • Timestamped feedback and approvals
  • Downloads unlocked only after rights are confirmed and the project is delivered

What is outside the scope

It does not stream music to the public, publish charts, or generate music.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱2,490 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.

Need SENZ to do the work with you?

Software access and professional services have separate scopes. For strategy, PR, creative direction or a custom implementation, discuss a service project.
