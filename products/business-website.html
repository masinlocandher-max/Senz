Who it is for

Small businesses, professionals, and organizations that need a clear web presence.

The problem it solves

The business has no simple website where people can see its services and send an inquiry.

What you can do

  • Services, highlights, about, photos, FAQ, and contact details on one page that you write and edit
  • An inquiry form whose messages arrive in your inbox with a reference
  • A page title and description for search engines, a SENZ web address, and a QR code

What is outside the scope

It is hosted at a SENZ web address: custom domains, online payments, and online stores are not included.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱1,490 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.

Need SENZ to do the work with you?

Software access and professional services have separate scopes. For strategy, PR, creative direction or a custom implementation, discuss a service project.
