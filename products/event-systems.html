Who it is for

Schools, organizations, and event organizers.

The problem it solves

Event sign-ups are manual: overbooking, lost registrations, and slow check-in at the door.

What you can do

  • A public registration page with capacity limits and an automatic waitlist; attendees check their status with their code
  • Single-use check-in codes that block duplicate entry
  • Attendee export and deletion of attendee data after the event

What is outside the scope

It does not sell tickets or run voting or tabulation.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱3,490 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.
