Who it is for

Cafés, restaurants, and food stalls.

The problem it solves

Printed menus go out of date, and guests cannot see what is available or suits their diet.

What you can do

  • Menu sections and items with prices, descriptions, photos, and dietary tags you enter
  • Mark an item sold out or available again; the public menu changes at once
  • A public menu page and a printable QR code; guests filter by dietary tag and build a list to show staff

What is outside the scope

Dietary information is entered by the business and is not checked by SENZ. It does not take orders or payments; guests show their list to staff.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱790 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.
