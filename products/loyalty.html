SENZ Loyalty

Digital Loyalty Stamp Cards.

Run a digital stamp card: customers earn stamps on purchases, and you record each reward when it is redeemed.

Product activation is awaiting release verification. Request availability or a demonstration; SENZ will confirm a working setup and activation date before requesting payment.

Who it is for

Cafés, salons, shops, and other businesses with repeat customers.

The problem it solves

Paper stamp cards get lost or copied, and the business cannot see who its regular customers are.

What you can do

  • One stamp program with the number of stamps, the reward, and an optional minimum spend that you set
  • Members join with their agreement; stamps come from POS sales or are added by hand with a note (by owners and administrators unless you allow all staff), and a full card is redeemed for the reward
  • Each member's card with a dated history of stamps and rewards and who recorded them

What is outside the scope

It sends no messages and has no public member page; members ask your staff for their card. A voided POS sale takes back its stamp.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱490 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.
