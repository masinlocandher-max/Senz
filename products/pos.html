SENZ POS and Inventory

Sales & Inventory Tracking.

Availability requests are open. SENZ will confirm when your product can be activated and what you will pay before sending payment instructions.

Who it is for

Cafés, shops, stalls, and small retailers.

The problem it solves

Counter sales are tallied by hand, stock runs out without warning, and the day's total never matches the cash drawer.

What you can do

  • Items with categories, prices, and stock counts; every restock and count is recorded with who made it
  • Ring up a sale with quantities, a discount, the payment method, and change for cash; stock goes down at once and an item cannot be sold past its stock
  • Daily totals by payment method, best sellers, low-stock alerts, voids that return stock, a printable sales slip, and CSV export

What is outside the scope

It records sales and stock only. It is not a BIR-registered POS, does not issue official receipts or invoices, and does not process card or e-wallet payments.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱1,490 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.
