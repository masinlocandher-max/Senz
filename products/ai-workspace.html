Who it is for

Professionals, small businesses, and teams who write every day.

The problem it solves

Drafting, summarizing, and organizing work takes too long.

What you can do

  • Seven drafting tools: email, summary, rewrite, action items, FAQ, plan, and comparison
  • Refine a draft, edit by hand, and restore any earlier version
  • Search your documents and export them as Markdown or plain text

What is outside the scope

Uses only what you provide. It does not browse the web, send, or publish anything, and every draft needs human review.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Seat count and any additional seat fees are confirmed in your quote. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.
