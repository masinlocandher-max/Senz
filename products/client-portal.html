Who it is for

SENZ clients working under a signed project agreement.

The problem it solves

Clients of a SENZ project need one place to follow progress, review deliverables, and approve the work.

What you can do

  • Milestones and deliverables with version history
  • Comments, revision requests, approvals, and client uploads
  • Final delivery with a record of who approved what and when

What is outside the scope

Set up by SENZ for each contract. It is not a general CRM, form builder, or public file-sharing service.

What happens after your request

  1. SENZ confirms your use case, availability, total price and activation date.
  2. You receive the agreed scope and payment instructions.
  3. SENZ verifies payment and activates the purchased license.
  4. You sign in to the SENZ App. For an organization, an administrator assigns the licensed seats.

Additional seats are ₱4,900 per seat/month. Setup, custom work and data migration are quoted separately. The website does not charge your card. Renewal requires a new verified payment.

Access and public pages stop when the license expires. Arrange exports and renewal before your end date.

Need SENZ to do the work with you?

Software access and professional services have separate scopes. For strategy, PR, creative direction or a custom implementation, discuss a service project.
