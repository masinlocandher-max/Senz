Working with SENZ

Where do I start?

With a short inquiry. SENZ usually suggests a free Authority Check of up to 30 minutes to find the gap that matters most, then recommends the smallest useful scope.

Can I commission only one service?

Yes. SENZ accepts focused strategy, messaging, creative, content, PR or digital projects when a standalone scope is appropriate. The recommendation follows the actual problem, not the number of services available.

Who does SENZ work with?

Founders, businesses, leaders, public-facing personalities, organizations, cultural initiatives, campaigns and teams that need clearer positioning and stronger communication.

What happens after I submit an inquiry?

SENZ reviews the goal, audience, timing and context. If there is a fit, SENZ may ask for more information, schedule a conversation or recommend a defined scope. A form submission does not confirm a meeting or project.

Are SENZ Digital products such as FotoLab already available?

They are product directions at different stages, from research to custom builds. SENZ confirms the current stage, scope and availability before any commitment. See Work & Demos for each status.

Can SENZ work with our existing team or suppliers?

Yes, when roles, approvals, access and accountability are clearly defined.

Pricing and process

How is pricing determined?

Pricing reflects the problem, strategic depth, deliverables, timeline, production, specialist support, revisions and ongoing involvement. Published rates are starting benchmarks; the final quotation follows scoping.

Is a down payment required?

Payment schedules are stated in the approved proposal or agreement. Work begins after the agreement, the initial payment and the requested materials are received.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope, the number of decision-makers, existing materials, approval speed and production. The schedule is stated in the proposal before work begins.

Does SENZ guarantee media coverage or business results?

No. SENZ improves strategy, clarity, execution and readiness. Third-party decisions, public reaction, platform performance, media interest and sales cannot be guaranteed unless a written agreement says otherwise.

SENZ Partners

What is SENZ Partners?

The partner program lets independent entrepreneurs build their own customer-facing business using eligible SENZ products and scoped production. You run your brand and customers; SENZ supplies approved work under written quotations.

Am I employed by SENZ or buying a franchise?

No. Partners are independent businesses. Membership is not employment, a franchise, a business registration, an exclusive territory or a licence to SENZ intellectual property.

What does ₱999 cover?

₱999 covers partner membership. Product licences, production, and system usage are priced separately. The full inclusions and the purchase terms, including duration, support scope, refund policy and total payable, are shown before checkout.

Is paid membership open?

Not yet. The program is in private preview. Checkout opens only after the purchase terms are published and payment verification is connected. You can request access now.

Which products can partners sell?

Only products marked Ready to Sell, within their white-label permission. Coming Soon and Availability to Confirm products may be discussed as concepts only, without promising delivery or taking payment.

Will SENZ provide customers or guarantee income?

No. Customers are not supplied and income is not promised. Results depend on your work, customer demand, approved products and costs.

General

Where is SENZ based?

BGC / Taguig, Philippines. Meetings are arranged after confirmation, online or at an agreed location.

Is the Creative Network a list of job openings?

No. It is a pool of independent creatives and specialists who may be contacted for suitable project-based work. Submitting a profile does not guarantee employment, a contract or immediate work.

How is information submitted through the website handled?

SENZ uses it to review and respond to inquiries or assess creative-network profiles. Details are in the privacy notice.
