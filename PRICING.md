# SENZ Commercial Offer Structure

SENZ uses a simple engagement ladder so clients can begin with low risk, pay for defined execution, and continue only when ongoing work creates value.

## 1. Digital Presence & Authority Check
**Free · Up to 30 minutes**

A diagnostic conversation covering the highest-priority issue affecting discovery, trust, conversion, reputation, or digital operations.

The Authority Check is diagnosis, not unpaid production. Its purpose is to determine whether a paid engagement is justified and what the smallest useful scope should be.

## 2. Focused Implementation
**Projects start at ₱25,000**

For a clearly defined problem that can be solved without a larger transformation.

Typical work may include:
- Website or landing-page implementation
- Positioning and messaging refinement
- Inquiry or booking journey improvement
- Search and discoverability foundations
- Campaign or communication system
- CRM, automation, or another scoped digital solution

### SENZ Launch
**Starting at ₱25,000**

Up to five pages, positioning session, mobile-first design, copy refinement, inquiry form, basic SEO, analytics, hosting, and 30 days of support.

### SENZ Business
**Starting at ₱55,000**

Up to ten pages, brand and messaging review, full copywriting, lead capture, booking, CRM integration, technical SEO, analytics, and 90 days of support.

## 3. Integrated Authority Build

### SENZ Authority
**Starting at ₱95,000**

Positioning workshop, reputation audit, premium website, strategic messaging, authority content, structured data, search and AI visibility foundations, and six months of priority support.

### SENZ Digital Platform
**Starting at ₱180,000**

Custom portals, user accounts, applications, appointments, document workflows, dashboards, notifications, role-based access, deployment, and training.

## 4. Ongoing Support

Ongoing work is offered when there is a clear maintenance, optimization, growth, or strategic need after launch.

### SENZ Care
**₱5,000 per month**

Maintenance, backups, security monitoring, minor updates, and monthly performance checks.

### SENZ Growth
**₱18,000 per month**

Maintenance, content updates, SEO monitoring, analytics, conversion recommendations, and monthly consultation.

### SENZ Authority Management
**₱45,000 per month**

Strategic communications, reputation monitoring, authority content, visibility improvements, reporting, and priority support.

## Pricing Rule

Published rates are starting benchmarks for planning and qualification, not fixed guaranteed quotations. Final pricing depends on strategy, scope, content, integrations, complexity, timeline, and support requirements.

SENZ should recommend the smallest commercially useful engagement that can solve the priority problem. Larger projects and retainers should be proposed only when the additional scope has a defensible business case.
