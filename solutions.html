Strategic communications and positioning

For organizations, founders and public figures whose message is unclear, inconsistent or easy to overlook.

Read more: Strategic communications · Brand strategy · Personal branding

What you receive

  • Positioning and audience priorities
  • Core messaging and narrative
  • A communication plan for a launch, relaunch or important moment
  • Guidance for spokespeople and leaders

Often starts with a positioning session after the free Authority Check.

PR and reputation

For brands and public-facing people who need attention without losing control of the story.

Read more: Public relations · Pageant PR · Handling negative comments

What you receive

  • Media strategy and press materials
  • Reputation review and risk points
  • Response preparation for difficult moments
  • Stakeholder and community messaging

Media coverage and public reaction depend on third parties and are never guaranteed.

Websites and digital presence

For businesses that need to be easier to find, understand, contact and choose.

Read more: Website strategy · Does a small business need a website?

What you receive

  • Mobile-first pages with clear, honest copy
  • Enquiry or booking path
  • Search and structured-data foundations
  • Analytics, hosting and a defined support period

Typical starting scope: SENZ Launch or SENZ Business (see benchmarks below).

Applications and business systems

For organizations losing time or opportunities because their work lives in chats, spreadsheets and memory.

What you receive

  • Client portals and user accounts
  • Booking, appointment and document workflows
  • Dashboards and notifications
  • Role-based access, deployment and training

Typical starting scope: SENZ Digital Platform, quoted after requirements review.

Content videos and marketing kits

For brands with something to promote but no consistent way to show it.

What you receive

  • Video concepts, scripts and production to an agreed format
  • Coordinated campaign materials around one offer
  • Brand-consistent formats for social, print and web
  • Usage rights and revision rounds agreed up front

Scoped per campaign. Quotation follows a short brief.

Scoped business automation

For teams that handle the same enquiries and handovers by hand every day.

What you receive

  • Process mapping before any tool is chosen
  • Enquiry routing and follow-up workflows
  • AI-assisted answers with human review
  • Integrations with defined data boundaries

Automation is scoped narrowly and kept human-reviewed. Savings are not promised before the process is measured.
