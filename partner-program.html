Not the right fit

Commercial clarity

Know what partnership does—and does not—mean.

Separate scopes

Program participation, product licences, system usage, setup and delivery services may have separate charges. Nothing is charged through this application.

No automatic approval

Applying does not create membership, product access, territory exclusivity or permission to make commitments on behalf of SENZ.

No income promise

Partners operate independent businesses. SENZ does not guarantee customers, sales, earnings or business success.

Important questions

Before you apply.

Is this employment with SENZ?

No. Partners operate independent businesses. The program does not create employment, a salary, benefits or authority to act as a SENZ employee.

Will SENZ give me customers?

No customers or income are guaranteed. Partners are responsible for developing their own market and customer relationships.

Can I sell every SENZ product?

No automatic right is granted. SENZ confirms which products are eligible, available and appropriate for each opportunity before a commitment is made.

What does it cost?

Program participation, product licences, setup, system usage and delivery services may have separate scopes and charges. These are explained and documented before payment. This application does not take payment.

Is this the Creative Network?

No. The Partner Program is for independent entrepreneurs building customer relationships and offers. The Creative Network is a separate route for specialists interested in project-based collaboration with SENZ.
