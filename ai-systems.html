How SENZ AI Access Works

Two authorized paths for accessing SENZ AI systems.

There is no free operational AI access on SENZ. Individual access requires a verified personal subscription. Institutional access requires a valid paid organizational license with an assigned seat. Both paths lead to the same products — the difference is who holds the commercial agreement.

Individual Subscription

For individuals, professionals, freelancers, and business owners purchasing access for themselves.

  • One named personal user
  • Direct verified payment
  • Personal workspace and projects
  • Defined usage allowances by plan
  • Self-managed account and billing
Request individual access

Institutional License

For schools, companies, government units, associations, and organizations purchasing access for their people.

  • Centrally purchased license
  • Named seats assigned by administrator
  • Organization workspace and controls
  • Usage reporting and audit
  • Negotiated scope, support and terms
Learn about institutional access
