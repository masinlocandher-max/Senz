IN DEVELOPMENT · USES AI

SENZ AI Workspace

Purpose: Turn your own notes, goals, and source text into usable first drafts, and keep every version.

For: Professionals, small businesses, and teams who write every day.

  • Seven drafting tools: email, summary, rewrite, action items, FAQ, plan, and comparison
  • Refine with AI, edit by hand, and restore any earlier version
  • Search your documents and export them as Markdown or plain text

Limits: Uses only what you provide. It does not browse the web, send, or publish anything, and every draft needs human review.

SENZ FotoLab

Limits: A self-service editor, not a retouching service by SENZ staff. It does not generate or alter images with AI.
