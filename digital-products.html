What does the monthly price cover?

Each product price covers one named user in one workspace, with the storage and processing allowance on its product page. Extra seats use the same per-seat rate. Setup and custom services are quoted separately.

How do I get access?

Request the tool you need. SENZ confirms availability, your total, any applicable taxes and an activation date before payment. After verified payment or an authorized agreement, your licensed tools open in SENZ App. Teams assign a named seat to each user.

There is no automatic card checkout or automatic renewal. Public pages must be published under an active license; arrange exports before your license ends.

How do processing credits work?

Drafting and answer tools have a monthly allowance. Longer inputs and outputs use more credits. Each credit covers up to US$0.01 of processing, rounded up per successful task. The app reserves enough for the task and returns unused credits. Failed tasks return the allowance. Credits reset each calendar month and do not roll over.

These tools generate drafts and automated answers. Review outputs before relying on them. Customer-facing representatives are disclosed as automated.

Can SENZ help with the strategy too?

Yes. SENZ professional services help shape your positioning, communication and digital experience. Software and human services have separate scopes, so you can choose the level of support you need.
