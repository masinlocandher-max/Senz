This website helps you choose and request a solution. The SENZ App is your protected workspace for using the products you license. Creating an account does not grant product access.

What the monthly price covers

The published product price covers one named user in one workspace and the listed storage and AI allowance. Additional seats use the same per-seat rate. Setup, custom development, data migration and human creative or strategic work are quoted separately. SENZ confirms the total payable amount, any applicable taxes, the term and activation date before payment.

There is no automatic card checkout or automatic renewal. Public menus, booking pages and catalogs remain visible only while published under an active license. Export your data before the license ends.

How AI credits work

Credits measure processing used, rather than promising unlimited generations. A longer input or output uses more credits. Each credit covers up to US$0.01 of model processing, rounded up per successful task. The app reserves enough for the requested task and returns unused credits. Failed tasks return the customer allowance. Credits reset each calendar month and do not roll over.
