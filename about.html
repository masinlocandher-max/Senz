About SENZ

Clarity is not simplification. It is disciplined thinking.

SENZ is the strategic communications and digital solutions brand within FMB&CO. Led by Francine Marie Bautista, our team helps businesses, leaders, and organizations strengthen their positioning, public presence, and digital operations.

Why SENZ Exists

Good ideas are often ignored because they are poorly framed.

A brand may have talent, purpose, and potential but still struggle when its audience cannot quickly understand what it stands for, why it matters, or what action to take. SENZ connects strategy, story, creative direction, public relations, and digital execution so every visible output supports one clear position.

Strategy-led

We define the problem, audience, position, and priorities before recommending execution.

Concept-driven

Creative work must carry meaning, strengthen recognition, and serve the goal.

Evidence-conscious

We avoid inflated claims and separate what is known, assumed, recommended, and still unverified.

Reputation-aware

We consider how each decision may affect trust, perception, relationships, and long-term value.

Founder & Creative Director

Francine Marie Bautista

Francine Marie Bautista is a Filipina strategic communications consultant from Masinloc, Zambales, Philippines, and founder of SENZ Strategic Communications and FMB&CO. She is also a brand strategist, public relations practitioner, creative director, storyteller, photographer, and trainer. Her work brings together positioning, communication, reputation, culture, identity, visibility, public perception, and digital products.

Her practice connects creative direction, communications, culture, and digital solutions.

She founded SENZ to help valuable people and ideas become easier to understand, harder to dismiss, and better prepared for public attention.
