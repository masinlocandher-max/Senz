03

Put SENZ App
within reach.

Add the web app to your phone or computer after your workspace is available.

Start from your SENZ workspace link. Install from the app itself. Adding this public website to your Home Screen will not open your client tools.

iPhone or iPad
  1. Open your workspace link in Safari.
  2. Open Share and choose Add to Home Screen. You may need to select More first.
  3. Turn on Open as Web App if shown, then tap Add.
Android
  1. Open your workspace link in Chrome.
  2. Choose Install SENZ App when offered. Or open Chrome’s menu, choose Install and create shortcut, then Install. Some versions call this Add to Home screen.
  3. Open SENZ App from your Home Screen and sign in.
Computer
  1. Open your workspace link in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Use the install icon in the address bar or the browser’s app installation menu.
  3. On a Mac with a supported Safari version, choose File, then Add to Dock.

Menu names vary by browser. If installation isn’t offered, use the workspace in your browser. An internet connection and confirmed access are required.
