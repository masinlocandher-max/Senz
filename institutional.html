What This Is

Licensing, not a free trial.

Institutional licensing means your organization enters a formal agreement with SENZ to deploy AI systems for a defined group of users. Access is centrally purchased, seats are individually assigned, and the institution — not individual employees — holds the license.

This is not a free pilot or an informal arrangement. It is a structured deployment with a signed agreement, a provisioned environment, and clear data handling terms. Each licensed user gets their own isolated workspace; the institution cannot access individual workspaces without the user's knowledge.

Institutional implementation status: All SENZ AI products are currently in development or custom implementation. Institutional licensing agreements can be initiated now — deployment begins when the relevant product reaches availability. Early adopters contribute to product requirements and receive priority provisioning.

Seat Assignment

Each licensed seat is assigned to a named individual. Seat transfers require authorization from the institution's designated SENZ administrator.

Tenant Isolation

Each user's workspace is isolated. Organization A cannot read from Organization B. Employer data and personal data never silently merge.

Data Privacy

How personal data is processed is set out in each written agreement and reviewed with your institution, together with its own data policies, before any personal data is processed. SENZ does not claim certification or regulatory compliance without verification.

Procurement Pathways

Government proposals require a separate review of procurement eligibility, required documents, privacy, and security. SENZ does not promise procurement eligibility or regulatory compliance without verification.

How It Works

From inquiry to deployment.

Institutional licensing follows a structured process. We do not rush provisioning — each step exists to protect your institution and your users.

1

Submit an inquiry

Tell us about your institution — size, sector, intended use, and timeline. No commitment required at this stage. We use this to determine whether SENZ is the right fit and which products apply to your situation.

2

Receive a proposal

We prepare a written proposal covering: which products apply, licensing terms, seat count, pricing structure, data processing terms, and a deployment timeline. The proposal includes any procurement documentation your office requires.

3

Review and sign agreement

Your legal or procurement office reviews the agreement. We accommodate standard government or corporate review timelines. Signing creates the institutional account and authorizes provisioning.

4

Provisioning and seat assignment

SENZ creates your institutional workspace, configures access controls, and assigns your administrator account. Your designated SENZ administrator then assigns individual seats to named staff. This step requires payment to be processed — no seats are provisioned on a promise of future payment.

5

Onboarding and support

Licensed institutions receive onboarding materials, administrator documentation, and a defined support channel. Product updates are included for the license term. Renewal is annual unless otherwise agreed.

Responsible AI

What we commit to.

Institutional AI deployment carries real responsibility. SENZ institutional licenses come with written commitments, not just aspirations.

No silent data merging

Your institution's data stays in your institution's environment. We do not train on your operational data without a separate written data processing agreement. Personal user data and organizational data are kept separate by design.

Honest product status

We will not represent a product as available when it is still in development. Your agreement will specify which products are being licensed and their current status at signing. Deployment timelines are estimates, not guarantees.

Clear access boundaries

Your institution's administrator can manage seat assignments. SENZ staff access institutional environments only for support purposes, with logged access and notification to your administrator.

Philippine law alignment

Applicable privacy, security, procurement, and sector requirements must be reviewed before a contract is finalized. Required safeguards and responsibilities will be documented for the agreed scope.
