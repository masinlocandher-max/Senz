# SENZ Brand Guide

## Identity and role
SENZ Strategic Communications and Digital Solutions is an FMB&CO brand.
FMB&CO is the parent brand; SENZ serves clients directly and operates the SENZ Partner Program for independent entrepreneurs.

## Positioning
Influence Through Clarity.
Public headline: Be found. Be trusted. Be chosen.
SENZ helps businesses become clearer, more credible, and easier to choose through strategic communications and digital solutions.

## Two distinct audiences
- senzpr.com: businesses hiring SENZ for defined projects and ongoing support.
- SENZ Partner Program: independent businesses serving their own customers through eligible products, resources and agreed delivery support.
- FMB&CO: parent-brand endorsement, not a third competing service offer.

Use “SENZ Partner Program” consistently. Reserve that label for the entrepreneurial program. Call direct-client recurring services “Ongoing Growth Support”. Creative Network is a separate specialist collaboration route.

## Commercial boundaries
Direct-client project fees and partner membership purchase different scopes. Do not imply membership includes software, unlimited production, employment or guaranteed customers. Product availability, licences and delivery need separate verification.
The Partner Program is an ongoing initiative, not a future-launch announcement. Lead with current business development, resources and customer conversations. Online paid checkout is unavailable in the reviewed source; describe that specific limitation without presenting the whole program as inactive. Do not claim payment or activation works until verified. The local About section is the public partner information route until the partner domain is verified.

## Voice and design
Strategic, warm, precise and human. Explain the customer outcome before the capability. Avoid inflated claims, internal revenue language and unsupported results.
Preserve the approved SENZ logo and existing layout. Core palette: navy #0A1730, blue #2451D9, cyan #8FD3FF. Use existing typography and components.

## Capabilities
Communications, PR, marketing, digital systems, imaging, music and creative kits are capabilities within SENZ. Do not present them as unrelated companies or require clients to navigate internal divisions.
