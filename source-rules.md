# SENZ content and evidence rules

Use AGENTS.md and source-of-truth.json for repository ownership. Use BRAND_GUIDE.md for positioning.
Verify service capabilities, product availability, pricing and commercial claims against approved records. Never invent client work, testimonials, coverage, earnings or results.
Label prototypes and illustrative examples clearly. A catalog record is not proof of an operational product.
Separate direct-client services from partner membership, licences, usage and delivery. Keep free and paid benefits accurate.
Obtain permission for customer names, assets and case studies. Keep credentials, private client information and personal data out of public source.
A commit or passing unit test is not proof of deployment, live form delivery, authentication or payment. Report each verification boundary honestly.
