# SENZ design rules

Serve businesses commissioning SENZ first. Keep one primary client inquiry action and a secondary Partner Program route.
Preserve the existing logo, palette, typography, service URLs, forms and assets unless an approved task changes them.
Present Digital Presence, Visibility & Reputation, and Digital Operations as customer outcomes. Explain scope before tools.
Identify SENZ as an FMB&CO brand. Send partner prospects to the About page’s partner-program section until the separate destination is verified.
Keep mobile navigation readable, links descriptive, keyboard focus visible and content accessible with enlarged text. Do not clip content to force a fixed-height screen.
Use actual work or clearly labelled illustrative imagery. Do not imply illustrations are client evidence.
Keep pricing, forms and production integrations unchanged during editorial work. Verify changes with the checks in AGENTS.md.
