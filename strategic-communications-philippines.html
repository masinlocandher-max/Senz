Frequently Asked Questions

What to know before you begin.

What is strategic communications?

It is the deliberate coordination of positioning, messaging, channels, public relations, content, and reputation around a defined goal and audience.

Does SENZ only create strategy documents?

No. SENZ can also translate the approved direction into messaging, creative direction, content systems, PR materials, and website work within an agreed scope.

Can SENZ support a launch or relaunch?

Yes. SENZ can scope a launch plan around your audience, message, channels and timing. Deliverables and pricing are confirmed in a project proposal.
